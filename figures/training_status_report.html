D8 — local_attn did not drift, it collapsed

This was being tracked as a ~21% train-loss rise. The full-range refetch shows the loss was the smaller half of the story: generative quality peaked and then fell away sharply, and the two events are coincident.

Sampling peaks around epoch 238–241, train loss bottoms at epoch 267, and the sampling collapse lands around epoch 285–290. A rising train loss rules out classical overfitting, and the cosine LR schedule was verified on-spec to four significant figures — so neither of the two obvious explanations survives. Still untested: the cluster-random sampler drawing progressively harder cluster members, and optimizer/EMA state drift across roughly twenty resumes.

The peak is recoverable. chk_epoch=00000237_step=000000097700.ckpt is a full 257 MB checkpoint with optimizer state, sitting essentially at the peak. The relaunch now running resumes from epoch 386 — that is, on the degraded plateau. Rewinding is a live option.
